# Trademarks and brand assets

The open-source licenses on this repository cover the code. They do not give anyone rights to the Cairn name or logo.

**Covered:** the name "Cairn" when used for an agent product or service, the three-stone logo, the wordmark, and everything in `assets/brand/`.

**You may, without asking:**

- Say that your project works with, or is compatible with, Cairn.
- Refer to Cairn by name when you write about it, review it or teach it.
- Run an unmodified copy of Cairn for yourself or your organization and call it Cairn.

**Please ask first, or pick your own name and logo, when you:**

- Publish a modified version, a fork or a hosted service.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your product is made, endorsed or sponsored by the Cairn project.
- Register the name, the logo or something confusingly close as a trademark, domain name or app-store listing.

A fork is welcome. It just needs its own name and logo, so users can tell the two apart.

The owner of the Cairn name and brand assets is Richard Mensah. The mark is not yet registered; registration is an open item in [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md). Get qualified advice before relying on this policy.
